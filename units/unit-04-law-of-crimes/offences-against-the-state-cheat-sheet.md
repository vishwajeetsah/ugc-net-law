# Cheat Sheet — Offences against the State (BNS ss. 147–158)

One-page revision sheet. Unit IV, UGC NET Law (Code 58). Legal information only — not legal advice.

## Section map (BNS ↔ IPC)

| BNS | IPC | Offence in one line | Max punishment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 147 | 121 | Waging war vs Govt of India (attempt/abetment same) | **Death** or life + fine |
| 148 | 121A | Conspiracy to commit s. 147 offence / overawe Govt by criminal force | Life or 10 yrs + fine |
| 149 | 122 | Collecting arms etc. / preparing to wage war | Life or 10 yrs + fine |
| 150 | 123 | Concealing a design to wage war, intending to facilitate it | 10 yrs + fine |
| 151 | 124 | Assaulting President/Governor to compel/restrain lawful power | 7 yrs + fine |
| **152** | — (124A deleted) | Endangering sovereignty, unity & integrity of India | Life or 7 yrs + fine |
| 153 | 125 | Waging war vs foreign State at peace with India | Life or 7 yrs + fine |
| 154 | 126 | Depredation on territory of such foreign State | 7 yrs + fine + forfeiture |
| 155 | 127 | Receiving property taken by war/depredation (ss. 153–154) | 7 yrs + fine + forfeiture |
| 156 | 128 | Public servant **voluntarily** lets prisoner of State/war escape | Life or 10 yrs + fine |
| 157 | 129 | Public servant **negligently** suffers such prisoner to escape | Simple imprisonment 3 yrs + fine |
| 158 | 130 | Anyone aiding escape / rescuing / harbouring such prisoner | Life or 10 yrs + fine |

## s. 152 vs s. 124A — the one comparison to memorise

| | s. 124A IPC (deleted) | s. 152 BNS |
|---|---|---|
| Target | Disaffection towards the **Government** | Secession, armed rebellion, subversive/separatist activity; **sovereignty, unity & integrity of India** |
| Mental element | Intention (implied) | **"Purposely or knowingly"** (express) |
| Means | Words, signs, visible representation | + **electronic communication, financial resources** |
| Max term | Life or 3 yrs | Life or **7 yrs** |

**Rule of thumb:** criticism of the Government ≠ s. 152. The conduct must point at secession / armed rebellion / the State's integrity.

## Case-law timeline

| Year | Case | Holding |
|---|---|---|
| 1950 | *Romesh Thappar v. State of Madras* | Only grave offences (rebellion, waging war) endanger "security of the State" under Art. 19(2) — not mere public-order breaches. |
| 1962 | *Kedar Nath Singh v. State of Bihar* (5 judges) | s. 124A **upheld but read down**: only acts with intention/tendency to create disorder or incite violence. |
| 1995 | *Balwant Singh v. State of Punjab* | Casual slogans ("Khalistan Zindabad") without incitement = **not** sedition. |
| 2005 | *State (NCT of Delhi) v. Navjot Sandhu* | Parliament-attack convictions for waging war (s. 121) and conspiracy (s. 121A) upheld. |
| 2022 | *S.G. Vombatkere v. Union of India* | s. 124A kept **in abeyance**; Union to reconsider; no fresh FIRs. |
| 2023 | BNS enacted | s. 124A deleted; **s. 152** takes its place. |

## Exam traps

- **s. 147 is the only death-penalty section in the chapter** — and attempt/abetment are punished on par with the completed act.
- **"Government of India" (s. 147) = Central Government only** — war against a State Government is not s. 147.
- **s. 156 vs s. 157:** same result (escape), different fault — voluntary → life/10 yrs; negligent → simple imprisonment up to 3 yrs. Classic MCQ pair.
- **Sanction:** prosecution under this chapter needs **previous sanction of the Central Government** — BNSS s. 222 (CrPC s. 196).
- **Forfeiture** appears only in ss. 154–155 in this chapter.
- **s. 149** is one of the rare offences of *preparation* — preparation is generally not punishable (cf. s. 310(4), preparation to commit dacoity).
