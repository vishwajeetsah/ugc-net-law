# Flashcards — Property Offences (BNS ss. 303–334)

Unit IV, UGC NET Law (Code 58). Term → one-line answer. Legal information only — not legal advice.

|---|---|---|
| 1 | Theft — s. 303(1) elements | Dishonest intention + movable property + out of another's possession + without consent + moving it |
| 2 | Theft — s. 303(2) punishment | Up to 3 years, or fine, or both |

| 4 | Theft — community service | First offence, property value below ₹5,000, property returned or value paid |

| 28 | CBT — s. 316(3), (4), (5) | Carrier/wharfinger/warehouse-keeper — 7 yrs; clerk/servant — 7 yrs; public servant/banker/merchant/agent — life or 10 yrs |
