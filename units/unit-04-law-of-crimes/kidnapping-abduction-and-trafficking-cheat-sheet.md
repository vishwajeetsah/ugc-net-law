# Cheat Sheet — Kidnapping, Abduction and Trafficking (BNS ss. 137–146)

One-page revision sheet. Unit IV, Law of Crimes. BNS in force 1 July 2024; IPC numbers are the pre-2024 equivalents.

## 2. Kidnapping vs abduction

| | Kidnapping (s. 137) | Abduction (s. 138) |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Child (male < 16, female < 18) or unsound mind; any person (from India) | Any person, any age |
| **Act** | Taking or enticing out of keeping | Compelling by force, or inducing by deceitful means, to go from any place |
| **Consent** | Guardian's consent is the test; minor's consent irrelevant | Consent obtained by deceit is no consent |
| **Punishment** | In itself — up to 7 years | Only via a purpose-section (ss. 87, 97, 140) |

## 3. The "taking" trio — must know

| Case | Year | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| ***S. Varadarajan v. State of Madras*** | 1965 | No "taking" where the minor leaves on her own and the accused merely allows her to accompany him |
| ***Thakorlal D. Vadgama v. State of Gujarat*** | 1973 | Persuading/inducing a minor to leave **is** "taking" |
| ***State of Haryana v. Raja Ram*** | 1973 | Merely allowing accompaniment, without taking or enticing, is not kidnapping |

## 4. The s. 140 purpose ladder — one section, four punishments

**Murder** (140(1)): life / 10 yrs → **Ransom** (140(2)): death or life → **Secret confinement** (140(3)): 7 yrs → **Grievous hurt / slavery** (140(4)): 10 yrs.
Memory hook: **ransom is the only death-penalty provision in the cluster**.

## 5. The s. 143 trafficking ladder

**7–10** (one person) → **10–life** (more than one person) → **10–life** (a minor) → **14–life** (more than one minor) → **life** (repeat offender).
Victim's consent is **immaterial** once any listed means (threat, force, abduction, fraud, abuse of power, inducement) is used. "Exploitation" expressly includes prostitution/sexual exploitation, forced labour, slavery/servitude, and removal of organs.

## 6. Forced labour — constitutional anchor

- **Art. 23** — traffic in human beings, begar and forced labour prohibited. **Art. 24** — children below 14 barred from hazardous work.
- ***PUDR v. Union of India* (1982)** — forced labour includes work for **less than the minimum wage** (economic compulsion counts).
- ***Bandhua Mukti Morcha v. Union of India* (1984)** — bonded labour violates Arts. 21 + 23; State must identify, release and rehabilitate.
- ***Vishal Jeet v. Union of India* (1990)** — directions for rescue and rehabilitation of trafficked children.
- **s. 143–144** inserted by the Criminal Law (Amendment) Act, **2013** (Justice Verma Committee).

## 8. Exam traps

1. **Adult "kidnapping"** — an adult cannot be kidnapped from lawful guardianship; taking an adult by force/deceit is **abduction** (s. 138).
2. **s. 137(2) vs s. 140(2)** — simple kidnapping: up to 7 years; kidnapping **for ransom**: death or life.
3. **s. 141 age trap** — girl **under 21**, boy **under 18** (not the same threshold).
4. **s. 143 vs s. 144** — s. 143 punishes the **trafficker** (7 yrs–life); s. 144 punishes the **knowing exploiter** (5–7 yrs).
5. **s. 145 vs s. 146** — habitual slave dealing (life/10 yrs) vs a single act of unlawful compulsory labour (up to 1 yr).
6. **s. 142** — the concealer takes the **same punishment** as the kidnapper.
7. **s. 139** — begging: 10 years; **maiming** the child: life.
