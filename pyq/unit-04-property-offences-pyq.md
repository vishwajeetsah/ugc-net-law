# PYQ Practice Set — Property Offences (BNS ss. 303–334)

Unit IV, UGC NET Law (Code 58). Ten previous-year-style multiple-choice questions on the day's topic, with an answer key and explanations. Legal information only — not legal advice.

## Questions

**Q1.** Under BNS s. 303, which of the following is NOT an essential element of theft?

(a) Dishonest intention
(b) Movable property
(c) Taking out of the possession of a person without consent
(d) Intention to permanently deprive the owner of the property

**Q2.** A takes B's file from the office, photocopies it overnight, and returns it the next morning without B noticing. Applying *Pyare Lal Bhargava v. State of Rajasthan* (1963), A is guilty of:

(a) No offence, because the file was returned
(b) Theft, because temporary deprivation with dishonest intention suffices
(c) Criminal breach of trust, because A was entrusted with the file
(d) Mischief, because the file's value was diminished

**Q3.** Which of the following statements about BNS s. 304 (snatching) is correct?

(a) Snatching is a form of robbery, punishable with rigorous imprisonment up to 10 years
(b) Snatching requires the offender to cause hurt to the victim
(c) Snatching is committed where the offender suddenly, quickly or forcibly seizes movable property in order to commit theft, and is punishable with up to 3 years
(d) Snatching existed under the IPC as s. 379A

**Q4.** Under BNS s. 308, the punishment for ordinary extortion (s. 308(2)) is imprisonment up to:

(a) 3 years
(b) 5 years
(c) 7 years
(d) 10 years

**Q5.** A threatens Z with death unless Z hands over ₹50,000, and Z, in fear, hands over the money at once. The offence is punishable under:

(a) BNS s. 308(4) — up to 7 years
(b) BNS s. 308(5) — up to 10 years
(c) BNS s. 308(3) — up to 2 years
(d) BNS s. 309(4) — robbery, up to 10 years

**Q6.** Five persons conjointly commit robbery. During the robbery, one of them commits murder. Under BNS s. 310(3), the punishment for every one of the five is:

(a) Rigorous imprisonment up to 10 years and fine
(b) Death, imprisonment for life, or rigorous imprisonment for not less than 10 years, and fine
(c) Imprisonment for life only
(d) Rigorous imprisonment for not less than 7 years

**Q7.** Which of the following pairs is WRONG?

(a) BNS s. 310(4) — preparation to commit dacoity — RI up to 10 years
(b) BNS s. 310(5) — assembling for the purpose of committing dacoity — RI up to 7 years
(c) BNS s. 311 — robbery with attempt to cause death or grievous hurt — RI not less than 7 years
(d) BNS s. 312 — attempt to commit robbery when armed with a deadly weapon — RI up to 7 years

**Q8.** Under BNS s. 314 (dishonest misappropriation of property), the punishment is imprisonment which shall:

(a) Extend up to 2 years, with no minimum
(b) Not be less than 6 months but may extend to 2 years, with fine
(c) Extend up to 3 years and fine
(d) Extend up to 5 years, or fine, or both

**Q9.** A bank manager, entrusted with a customer's fixed deposit, dishonestly diverts the money to his own account. He is liable under BNS s. 316(5) (criminal breach of trust by a banker) with punishment of:

(a) Up to 5 years, or fine, or both
(b) Up to 7 years and fine
(c) Imprisonment for life, or up to 10 years, and fine
(d) Up to 3 years, or fine, or both

**Q10.** Under BNS s. 324 (mischief), a person who commits mischief causing loss or damage of ₹1 lakh or upwards is punishable under s. 324(5) with imprisonment up to:

(a) 6 months
(b) 1 year
(c) 2 years
(d) 5 years

## Answer key

| Q | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **(d)** | Theft requires dishonest intention at the time of taking — not an intention to *permanently* deprive. Temporary taking with dishonest intention is theft (*Pyare Lal Bhargava*, 1963). All of (a), (b) and (c) are elements of s. 303(1). |
| 2 | **(b)** | *Pyare Lal Bhargava*: taking a file temporarily to copy it is theft. The dishonest intention at the time of taking decides; the later return is irrelevant. |
| 3 | **(c)** | s. 304 is a **new** BNS offence (no IPC equivalent). It is a form of theft — sudden, quick or forcible seizure of movable property — punishable with up to 3 years and fine. Force against the *person* would make it robbery (s. 309). |
| 4 | **(c)** | **7 years.** This is a BNS change: IPC s. 384 capped ordinary extortion at 3 years. BNS s. 308(2) raised it to 7 years, or fine, or both. |
| 5 | **(b)** | The extortion was **completed** by fear of death/grievous hurt — s. 308(5), up to 10 years. s. 308(4) (7 years) covers the *threat made in order to* commit extortion, where no delivery follows. It is not robbery (s. 309) because there was no fear of *instant* death/hurt in the victim's presence inducing delivery then and there. |
| 6 | **(b)** | s. 310(3): where any one of the five commits murder in committing dacoity, **every one** of the five is punishable with death, life, or RI not less than 10 years, and fine. |
| 7 | **(d)** | s. 312 prescribes RI **not less than 7 years** (a minimum sentence, like s. 311) — not "up to 7 years". (a), (b) and (c) are all correct: preparation 10 years, assembling 7 years, s. 311 minimum 7 years. |
| 8 | **(b)** | BNS s. 314 (IPC s. 403) now carries a **minimum of 6 months** — "imprisonment of either description for a term which shall not be less than six months but which may extend to two years and with fine". The minimum is new under the BNS. |
| 9 | **(c)** | s. 316(5): CBT by a public servant, or in the way of business as a banker, merchant, factor, broker, attorney or agent — imprisonment for life, or up to 10 years, and fine. General CBT (s. 316(2)) is up to 5 years; clerk/servant (s. 316(4)) is up to 7 years. |
| 10 | **(d)** | s. 324(5): loss/damage of ₹1 lakh or upwards — up to **5 years**. The ladder: (2) 6 months; (3) any property incl. Government/Local Authority — 1 year; (4) ₹20,000–<₹1 lakh — 2 years; (5) ₹1 lakh+ — 5 years. |
