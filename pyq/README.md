# Previous-Year Questions (PYQ) — Index and Analysis

## Official source (download papers here)

Question papers are released by the **National Testing Agency (NTA)** after each cycle, with the provisional answer keys.

- UGC NET official website: https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in — see the Archive / Downloads sections
- NTA main site: https://www.nta.ac.in

Always verify a paper's subject (Law, Code 58), date and shift before practising from any third-party copy.

## Weightage pattern (synthesis — NTA prescribes no fixed weightage)

- **Units I + II (Jurisprudence + Constitutional/Administrative): ~25–40% combined.** Priority 1.
- **Units III, IV, VI** (International Law/HR, Crimes, Commercial): the next tier.
- **Units V, VII, VIII, IX, X**: smaller shares but volatile — a "small" unit can jump in any cycle (Environment did in June 2023).
- Format pattern: matching sets, chronology / "arrange as in the Act" questions, assertion–reason, multi-statement — and **two comprehension sets at Q. 91–100** in every recent paper.
- Difficulty: easy-to-moderate in 2024 → sustained **moderate-to-difficult** from June 2025.

## Honest limitations

- The March 2023 phase's specific Law questions could not be verified from any published solved paper.
- June 2026 has no official solved paper at the time of writing; only memory-based analyses exist, and at least one circulating unit-wise table for that cycle is unreliable.
- Unit counts are approximations compiled from published solved papers and analyses; different sources classify overlapping questions differently.
