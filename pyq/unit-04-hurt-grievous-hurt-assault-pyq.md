# PYQ Practice Set — Hurt, Grievous Hurt and Assault (BNS ss. 114–136)

10 previous-year-style MCQs with answer key and explanations. Covers Unit IV, Chapter VI of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. Answers are at the end — attempt the questions first.

## Questions

**Q1.** Under BNS s. 114, a person is said to cause "hurt" when he causes:
(a) Bodily pain, disease or infirmity
(b) Mental pain or anguish only
(c) Loss of reputation
(d) Wrongful loss of property

**Q2.** Which of the following is **NOT** one of the eight kinds of grievous hurt under BNS s. 116?
(a) Emasculation
(b) Permanent privation of the sight of either eye
(c) Fracture or dislocation of a bone or tooth
(d) Hurt causing the sufferer severe bodily pain for ten days

**Q3.** A voluntarily causes hurt to B, intending to cause grievous hurt, and B in fact suffers grievous hurt. A is liable for voluntarily causing grievous hurt under:
(a) BNS s. 115
(b) BNS s. 117
(c) BNS s. 118
(d) BNS s. 122

**Q4.** Under BNS s. 117(3), where grievous hurt caused in the course of the offence leaves the victim in a permanent disability or a persistent vegetative state, the punishment is:
(a) Imprisonment up to 7 years and fine
(b) Rigorous imprisonment of not less than 10 years, which may extend to imprisonment for life
(c) Death or imprisonment for life
(d) Imprisonment up to 3 years and fine

**Q5.** A group of six persons acting in concert beats up B because of his caste, fracturing his arm. Each member of the group is guilty of causing grievous hurt under:
(a) BNS s. 103(2)
(b) BNS s. 117(4)
(c) BNS s. 118(2)
(d) BNS s. 124(1)

**Q6.** Voluntarily causing grievous hurt by dangerous weapons or means is punishable under BNS s. 118(2) with:
(a) Imprisonment up to 3 years and fine
(b) Imprisonment up to 7 years and fine
(c) Imprisonment for life or imprisonment up to 10 years, and fine
(d) Imprisonment up to 10 years and fine only

**Q7.** Which section of the BNS deals with voluntarily causing grievous hurt by use of acid?
(a) s. 123
(b) s. 124
(c) s. 125
(d) s. 126

**Q8.** A blocks the only road leading out of B's village so that B cannot proceed in a direction in which he has a right to proceed. A has committed:
(a) Wrongful confinement under s. 127
(b) Wrongful restraint under s. 126
(c) Assault under s. 130
(d) Criminal force under s. 129

**Q9.** Which of the following statements about assault under BNS s. 130 is **correct**?
(a) Mere words amount to assault
(b) Assault requires actual physical contact with the victim
(c) A gesture or preparation intending to cause apprehension of immediate criminal force amounts to assault
(d) A threat to use force next week amounts to assault

**Q10.** A confines B in a room for 12 days. The maximum punishment under BNS s. 127(4) is imprisonment up to:
(a) 1 year
(b) 2 years
(c) 3 years
(d) 5 years

## Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **(a)** | s. 114: "bodily pain, disease or infirmity". Mental pain alone is not hurt; loss of reputation or property falls under the wider s. 2(14) definition of "injury", not "hurt". |
| 2 | **(d)** | s. 116, kind 8 requires the sufferer to be in severe bodily pain or unable to follow ordinary pursuits for **twenty** days, not ten. All eight kinds are listed in the note. |
| 3 | **(b)** | s. 117(1): the accused both intended (or knew he was likely to cause) grievous hurt **and** grievous hurt was in fact caused. s. 115 is simple hurt; s. 118 needs dangerous weapons; s. 122 is the provocation provision. |
| 4 | **(b)** | s. 117(3) — a **new** BNS provision: rigorous imprisonment of not less than 10 years, which may extend to imprisonment for life (life = remainder of natural life). |
| 5 | **(b)** | s. 117(4) — group of five or more acting in concert causing grievous hurt on an identity ground; each member is guilty and punishable with up to 7 years and fine. s. 103(2) is the parallel provision for **murder** (mob lynching), not hurt. |
| 6 | **(c)** | s. 118(2) (IPC s. 326): imprisonment for life or imprisonment up to 10 years, and fine. s. 118(1) (simple hurt by dangerous weapons) is up to 3 years — the classic trap is swapping the two. |
| 7 | **(b)** | s. 124: (1) grievous hurt by acid — minimum 10 years to life; (2) throwing or attempting to throw acid — 5 to 7 years. s. 125 is the rash-or-negligent act endangering life; s. 123 is hurt by poison to commit an offence. |
| 8 | **(b)** | s. 126: voluntarily obstructing a person from proceeding in a direction he has a right to proceed is wrongful **restraint**. Confinement (s. 127) needs restraint **within circumscribing limits** — a locked room, not a blocked road. |
| 9 | **(c)** | s. 130: assault is a gesture or preparation causing apprehension of **immediate** criminal force. Mere words do not amount to assault (though words may give meaning to gestures), no contact is needed, and a threat of future violence is not assault. |
| 10 | **(d)** | s. 127(4): wrongful confinement for **ten days or more** — up to 5 years and fine of not less than ₹10,000. The ladder is 1 year (base) → 3 years (3+ days) → 5 years (10+ days). |
