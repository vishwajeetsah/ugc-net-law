# UGC NET Law (Subject Code 58) — Preparation Notes

Unit-wise study notes, previous-year question (PYQ) analysis and revision trackers for the **UGC NET Law Paper 2**, built and maintained by **Vishwajeet Sah** as part of his own preparation for the December 2026 cycle.

Paper 2 carries **100 questions / 200 marks** across 10 units. There is no research-methodology unit in Paper 2 — research and teaching aptitude are tested in Paper 1.

## What the PYQ analysis shows (Nov 2021 – Jun 2026)

- Units **I + II together account for roughly 25–40%** of Paper 2 in most cycles — they are Priority 1.
- Every recent paper ends with **two 5-question comprehension sets (Q. 91–100)**.
- Recurring themes: the amendment-case chronology (Shankari Prasad → Golaknath → Kesavananda Bharati), Hart–Austin, mens rea and the IPC/BNS Section 300 exceptions, Uniting for Peace, the M.C. Mehta / polluter-pays line, and "arrange in order" questions on divorce and maintenance grounds.
- Since June 2025 the paper tests the **new criminal codes (BNS / BNSS / BSA)** alongside the IPC / CrPC / Evidence Act — both must be studied.
- Difficulty has moved from easy-to-moderate (2024) to a sustained **moderate-to-difficult** (June 2025 onwards).
